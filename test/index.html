FAQ

Ferrand is a DJ and producer based in Cologne, Germany, making trance and hard dance built for peak time — Loveparade synths and hard house weight pulled into something that belongs right now, not in 1998.

Trance, hard dance and eurodance revival, played at around 150–155 BPM.

Ferrand's music has been supported by Marlon Hoffstadt, Armin van Buuren, KI/KI and Hannah Laing.

From Grelle Forelle in Vienna to Odonien in Cologne.

On Spotify and SoundCloud. Tour dates are listed on Resident Advisor.

Booking enquiries go to info.ferrand@gmail.com.
