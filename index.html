About

„Supported by Marlon Hoffstadt, Armin van Buuren, KI/KI, Hannah Laing."

It started with illegal raves in the woods and a Tomorrowland aftermovie at fourteen. Old Loveparade synths, the rawness of hard house — without really trying, he'd ended up exactly where he was always going.

His records have been picked up by Marlon Hoffstadt, KI/KI, Armin van Buuren, Hannah Laing, Mischluft, 2Hot2Play. He's played Lokschuppen Berlin, Grelle Forelle Vienna, Odonien and Artheater Cologne.

Cologne, Germany
